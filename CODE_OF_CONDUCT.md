# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is
best for the community. Harassment, trolling, personal attacks and publishing others' private
information are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private
[GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/Kiranchaudhary537/1click-wallet-transfer/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
Maintainers may remove comments or contributions, or ban contributors, who break these rules.
